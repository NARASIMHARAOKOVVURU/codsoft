What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals pretend to be trusted organizations to steal passwords, banking details, OTPs, or personal information using fake emails, websites, or messages.

How to Identify Phishing Attempts

📧 Suspicious Emails

  • Unknown sender
  • Spelling and grammar mistakes
  • Urgent messages like "Account will be blocked"
  • Unexpected attachments
  • Strange links

🔑 Fake Login Pages

  • Wrong website URL
  • No HTTPS security
  • Poor website design
  • Requests OTP immediately
  • Unexpected login requests

🌐 Fraudulent Websites

  • Misspelled domain names
  • Fake offers or prizes
  • No contact information
  • Too many pop-up ads
  • Untrusted payment pages
✔ Safety Tips
  • Always verify the sender's email.
  • Hover over links before clicking.
  • Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  • Keep antivirus software updated.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
⚠ Warning
If you receive a suspicious email or website, do not click any links. Report it to your IT administrator or email provider immediately.
